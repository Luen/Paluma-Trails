## Townsville Bushwalking Club

- Luen Warneke confirmed permission from **Wilfred Karnoll** to use information
  from the Townsville Bushwalking Club.
- Keep report titles, trip dates, author credits where given and source URLs.
  The Shay's additions use original summaries of the October 1965 report,
  June 1976 report and September 1976 newsletter.
- No club photograph or sketch map is included in this change. The club's
  historical reports are evidence of observations at those dates, rather than
  confirmation of current access, track condition or surviving fruit trees.
