## Editorial approach

The new POI descriptions are original factual summaries, with offline-readable
source labels. Existing IDs, coordinates and `unverified` flags are retained.
Gard is the spelling used in the book; the map's Garde IDs are permanent.
Shay's is described as a guesthouse; the book does not establish a licensed
hotel. No modern access rights, track condition, surviving structures, or
mobile reception are inferred from historical accounts.

The Garde clearing and lookout entries explicitly distinguish regional family
history from identification of a particular hut or lookout. Witt's lower and
upper markers receive area context only: the book does not locate the wartime
station at either individual marker. Johnstone's hut photograph is not a
photograph of Garde's hut. Cavill's battery is not Gards' Battery.
