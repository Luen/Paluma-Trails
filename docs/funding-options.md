## Separate the one-off pilot from recurring costs

Prepare two budgets rather than assuming a project grant will fund operations:

| Budget | Items to quote | Evidence to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-off signage pilot | Approved sign artwork, fabrication, mounting materials, authorised installation, accessible printable map, documented field testing | Land-manager approval (#27/#44), sign count and locations, supplier quotes, image/content permissions, installation responsibility and repair plan |
| Hosting and maintenance | Chosen hosting/domain plan, storage and traffic allowance, backups, monitoring, renewal and annual maintenance time | Hosting decision (#69), current supplier prices/terms, named payer and technical owner, renewal dates and cost ceiling |

Amounts are deliberately uncosted until quantities, permissions and supplier
quotes exist. Current web release archives and container images are available,
but publishing one is not a promise of free permanent hosting. For recurring
costs, an owner-funded plan or a separately agreed community/local-business
sponsorship is a possibility to discuss, not an existing offer. Record duration,
renewal, ownership, acknowledgement and exit arrangements before accepting it.
